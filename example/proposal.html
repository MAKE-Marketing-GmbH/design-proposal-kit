The directions Studio Example
Three ways to go

PICK A FEELING

Same structure, same words, three different worlds. Look at which one you would want to walk into.

A
Warm and playful
Cream paper, a serif headline, soft corners. Reads like a neighbourhood place that has been there a while.
B
Bright and photographic
White, lots of air, one green accent. The photography does the talking. Closest to a specialty shop.
C
Bold and colourful
Dark page, bright tiles, one yellow. Loud, confident, harder to forget. Least like the others.

Open points Studio Example
Only the gaps

WHAT WE
STILL NEED

Most of it is already in your brief. These are the few things we could not answer from it, and they are the reason some text above is a placeholder.

Photos
  • Do you have photos of the roastery and the drum?
  • Can we photograph the Saturday opening?
  • Are there portraits of the two of you?
Beans
  • How often does the bean list actually change?
  • Who edits it, and with what tool today?
Ordering
  • Should people order online, or only enquire?
  • Is wholesale a separate route with its own page?
Practical
  • Exact Saturday opening hours?
  • One language, or also a second?

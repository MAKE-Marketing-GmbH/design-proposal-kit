# Direction B: Bright and photographic

The checkable version of `00-PASTE-ME.txt`. See
`../warm-and-playful/PROMPT.md` for the fully annotated example; this one is
kept short.

## Where the facts come from

Every fact is invented. Northwind Coffee Roasters is fictional. In a real bundle
each line names its source, and a fact without one goes on the "What we still
need" page instead of into the paste block.

## Why this direction exists

Aimed at the buyer who reads tasting notes. White and air say "specialty", and
the photography carries the whole thing. It is the safest of the three, and the
easiest to get wrong: with this little decoration, a weak photo has nowhere to
hide.

If the client picks it, they are committing to real photography.

## Palette and roles

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` true white |
| Ink, headlines | `#14181A` |
| Body text | `#5A6468` cool grey |
| Accent | `#1F6F4A` green, used only for buttons and eyebrows |

## Deliberately excluded

Same hard exclusions as direction A: no prices, no faces, no ratings, no badges,
no press logos, no exact opening hours. None of it is in the brief.

The hero is told explicitly not to split 50/50, because that is the default the
model reaches for and it makes every site look the same.

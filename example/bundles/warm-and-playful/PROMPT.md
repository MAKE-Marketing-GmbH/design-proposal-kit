used to demonstrate this kit. In a real bundle every line below would name its

does not go in the paste block; it goes on the "What we still need" page.

## Why this direction exists

Aimed at the customer who buys coffee as a small pleasure rather than a
specification. The serif and the cream paper say "this place has been here a
while", which is the one thing a roastery can claim that a supermarket cannot.

It is the warmest of the three on purpose. If the client picks it, they are
telling us the brand is about the place, not the process.

## Palette and roles

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | `#F6EFE4` cream, warm, never white |
| Ink, headlines | `#2E241C` near-black with brown in it |
| Body text | `#6B5B4B` warm grey, softer than the ink |
| Accent, buttons and eyebrows | `#B4552D` terracotta, one accent only |

One accent. If a second colour appears, the direction has drifted.

## Deliberately excluded

- **Prices.** Not in the brief, and a price on a mockup gets read as a promise.
- **Faces.** No rights cleared for anyone.
- **Star ratings, badges, press logos.** None exist. Inventing social proof on a
  proposal is the fastest way to lose trust when the client notices.
- **Exact opening hours.** The brief says "Saturday morning" and nothing more, so
  the mockup says exactly that. The precise hours are an open question on the
  "What we still need" page.

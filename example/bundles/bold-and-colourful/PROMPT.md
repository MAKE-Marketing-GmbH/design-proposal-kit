# Direction C: Bold and colourful

The checkable version of `00-PASTE-ME.txt`. See
`../warm-and-playful/PROMPT.md` for the fully annotated example; this one is
kept short.

## Where the facts come from

Every fact is invented. Northwind Coffee Roasters is fictional. In a real bundle
each line names its source, and a fact without one goes on the "What we still
need" page instead of into the paste block.

## Why this direction exists

The deliberate outlier. Every set of three needs one that is genuinely different,
or the client is choosing between shades of the same idea and the exercise is
theatre.

Dark, type-led, colour tiles instead of photos. It is the only direction that
works without good photography, which makes it the honest fallback if the answer
to "do you have photos" turns out to be no.

## Palette and roles

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | `#12100F` near-black, flat, no gradient |
| Ink, headlines | `#F7F3EC` warm off-white |
| Body text | `#B9B0A4` |
| Accent | `#E8B33C` yellow |
| Tiles | `#E8B33C`, `#C4553A`, `#4F7A5B` |

The tiles are the one place a second and third colour are allowed, and only
there.

## Deliberately excluded

Same hard exclusions as the other two. Plus, specific to a dark page: no glow, no
neon rim light, no gradient haze. Those are the reflexes a model reaches for the
moment a design goes dark, and they are what make it read as AI output.
